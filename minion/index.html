FAQs

Everything you need to know

Is your cat food vet-approved?

Yes, all our formulas are developed with veterinarians and meet international nutritional standards for feline health.

What ingredients do you use?

We use high-quality proteins, natural ingredients, and essential vitamins. Our recipes contain no artificial colors, preservatives, or low-grade fillers.

Is your food suitable for cats with sensitivities?

Yes. We offer grain-free, hypoallergenic, and single-protein options designed for cats with food sensitivities or digestive issues.

How should I transition my cat to your food?

We recommend switching gradually over 7 days, mixing increasing amounts of our food with your cat’s current diet.

Do you offer food for all life stages?

Absolutely. We produce formulas for kittens, adult cats, and seniors, ensuring balanced nutrition at every stage of life.
